Current status

Transparent by design.

This is a working prototype, not a certified public-administration platform. We prefer to state the boundary clearly before a technical evaluation.

  • The core app, database and AI providers are live.
  • Optional social publishing needs each platform's approval and credentials.
  • Security, privacy, accessibility and procurement requirements can be scoped with your team.
  • No user numbers or government references are claimed without verification.
